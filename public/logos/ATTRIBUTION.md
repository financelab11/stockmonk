# Logo attribution

Company logos in this folder are used for identification only. They were
downloaded once at build time from the CompaniesMarketCap logo CDN
(`https://companiesmarketcap.com/img/company-logos/64/{ticker}.png`) and are
served locally; the site does not hotlink them at runtime.

Company names and logos are trademarks of their respective owners. Their
inclusion does not imply endorsement, sponsorship or affiliation with
Stockmonk.

If a logo is missing, outdated or removed at a company's request, the UI falls
back to a typographic monogram. To replace a logo, drop a square PNG or WebP at
`/public/logos/{TICKER}.png` and keep the file under 6 KB if possible.
