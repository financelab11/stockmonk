# Reference material

The task brief included screenshots of the reference site's home, discover,
all-stocks, newsfeed, framework and two research-article layouts, plus the brand
asset. The brand asset is stored here as `stockmonk-logo.png`; the derived app
icons, header mark and hero image live in `public/brand/` and `public/icons/`.

The screenshots were studied for information architecture, section order,
spacing, card anatomy, article density and responsive behaviour. No third-party
copy, images, testimonials, performance claims or analyst bios were copied into
the application. All Stockmonk copy and visuals are original or generated from
the project's own data.

If the screenshot files are added later, keep them in this folder; they are not
served by the app and are excluded from the PWA precache.
